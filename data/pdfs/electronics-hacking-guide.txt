1. BASIC ELECTRONICS
====================

OHM'S LAW (Fundamental):
V = I × R
- V = Voltage (Volts)
- I = Current (Amperes)
- R = Resistance (Ohms)

Power: P = V × I = I²R = V²/R

COMPONENTS:

Resistor:
- Limits current flow
- Color code:
  * Black=0, Brown=1, Red=2, Orange=3, Yellow=4
  * Green=5, Blue=6, Violet=7, Gray=8, White=9
  * Gold=±5%, Silver=±10%
- Example: Brown-Black-Red-Gold = 10×10² = 1000Ω ±5%

Capacitor:
- Stores electrical charge
- Types: Ceramic, electrolytic, tantalum
- Rating: Capacitance (Farads) + Voltage
- Polarity critical (electrolytic)

Diode:
- One-way current flow
- Anode (+) to Cathode (-)
- Types: Standard, Zener, LED, Schottky

Transistor:
- Electronic switch/amplifier
- NPN vs PNP
- Pins: Base, Collector, Emitter
- Common: 2N2222, 2N3904, BC547

Integrated Circuits:
- 555 Timer (most versatile)
- 7805 Voltage Regulator (5V)
- LM317 (adjustable regulator)
- Arduino/microcontrollers

TOOLS:

Essential:
- Multimeter (voltage, current, resistance, continuity)
- Soldering iron (30-60W)
- Wire strippers
- Diagonal cutters
- Breadboard (prototyping)

Advanced:
- Oscilloscope (signal analysis)
- Function generator
- Logic analyzer
- Hot air rework station

READING SCHEMATICS:

Symbols:
- ─────  Wire
- ─┬─   Junction (connected)
- ─┼─   Crossing (not connected)
- ─●─   Ground
- ─|─   Battery/DC source
- ═══   Capacitor
- ─∿─   Resistor
- ─>|─  Diode
- ─▷─   LED

2. POWER GENERATION & STORAGE
====================

SOLAR PANELS:

Types:
- Monocrystalline: 15-20% efficient, expensive
- Polycrystalline: 13-16% efficient, cheaper
- Thin film: 10-12% efficient, flexible

Sizing:
- Daily consumption: Sum all watt-hours
- Example: 100W light × 5 hours = 500Wh/day
- Panel size: Daily Wh ÷ Sun hours ÷ 0.75
- Example: 500 ÷ 5 ÷ 0.75 = 133W panel needed

Charge Controller:
- PWM (cheaper, less efficient)
- MPPT (expensive, 20-30% more efficient)
- Prevents overcharge/overdischarge

BATTERIES:

Lead-Acid:
- Flooded (maintenance required)
- AGM/Gel (sealed, maintenance-free)
- Deep cycle vs Starting
- 50% DOD (Depth of Discharge) max for longevity

Lithium:
- LiFePO4 (safest, longest life)
- Li-ion (energy dense, fire risk)
- 80-90% DOD safe
- 3-5x more expensive

Capacity:
- Measured in Amp-hours (Ah)
- Wh = Ah × Voltage
- Example: 100Ah × 12V = 1200Wh

Battery Bank Sizing:
1. Daily consumption (Wh)
2. Days of autonomy (2-3 typical)
3. DOD limit (50% lead, 80% lithium)
4. Calculation: (Daily Wh × Days) ÷ DOD ÷ Voltage = Ah

WIND TURBINE:

DIY Design:
- Car alternator (12V/24V)
- PVC pipe blades or wood
- Tail vane for direction
- Tower height critical (wind exponential)

Power Formula:
P = 0.5 × ρ × A × V³ × Cp
- ρ = air density (~1.225 kg/m³)
- A = swept area (πr²)
- V = wind velocity
- Cp = efficiency (~0.4 max)

INVERTER:

Pure Sine Wave vs Modified:
- Pure: Clean power, expensive, runs everything
- Modified: Square wave, cheap, some devices won't work

Sizing:
- Continuous watts + Surge capacity
- Example: Fridge 200W continuous, 800W surge
- Need 800W+ rated inverter

FUEL CELLS:
- Hydrogen + Oxygen → Electricity + Water
- Requires hydrogen source (dangerous)
- Experimental/advanced

3. RADIO COMMUNICATIONS
====================

FREQUENCY BANDS:

HF (High Frequency): 3-30 MHz
- Long distance (skip off ionosphere)
- Ham radio: 40m, 20m, 10m bands
- Requires license (most countries)
- Can reach globally

VHF (Very High Frequency): 30-300 MHz
- Line of sight
- FM broadcast (88-108 MHz)
- Ham 2m band (144-148 MHz)
- Aviation (118-137 MHz)
- Marine (156-162 MHz)

UHF (Ultra High Frequency): 300-3000 MHz
- Line of sight, shorter range than VHF
- Ham 70cm band (420-450 MHz)
- FRS/GMRS (462-467 MHz)
- Cell phones (varies by country)

AMATEUR RADIO (HAM):

License Classes (USA):
- Technician: VHF/UHF + limited HF
- General: Most HF privileges
- Extra: All frequencies

Equipment:
- Handheld (HT): 1-5W, portable
- Mobile: 25-50W, vehicle
- Base: 100W+, home station

Antennas:
- Dipole: Simple, effective (length = 468/freq in MHz)
- Vertical: Omnidirectional
- Yagi: Directional, high gain
- Ground plane critical for verticals

Modes:
- FM (Voice): Simplest, most common VHF/UHF
- SSB (Single Sideband): HF voice, efficient
- CW (Morse Code): Most efficient, longest range
- Digital: FT8, PSK31, RTTY

BUILDING ANTENNAS:

Half-Wave Dipole:
- Length: 468 / frequency(MHz) = feet
- Example: 146 MHz → 468/146 = 3.2 feet total
- Cut wire, center insulator, feedline

Quarter-Wave Ground Plane:
- Vertical element: 234 / freq(MHz) = feet
- 4 radials at 45° (same length)
- Coax center to vertical, shield to radials

LONG WIRE:
- Random wire as long as possible
- Antenna tuner required
- Simple HF antenna

SDR (Software Defined Radio):

Receive:
- RTL-SDR dongle ($25)
- Receives 24-1766 MHz
- Computer + software (GQRX, SDR#)
- Can decode: FM, AM, SSB, digital modes

Applications:
- Monitor police/fire (if legal)
- Aircraft tracking (ADS-B)
- Weather satellite images (NOAA)
- Ham radio monitoring
- SIGINT

Transmit SDR:
- HackRF One (half-duplex)
- LimeSDR (full duplex)
- GNU Radio software
- ⚠️ Illegal to transmit without license

ENCRYPTION:

Legal:
- AES encryption (not on ham radio - illegal)
- Data modes can be encrypted (non-ham)
- Commercial radios (business band) allow encryption

Voice Scrambling:
- Analog inversion (weak, easily broken)
- Digital voice (DMR, P25, NXDN)
- Some digital modes have AES option

Operational Security:
- Brevity codes
- Prearranged messages
- Frequency hopping (advanced)
- Directional antennas (harder to locate)

DIRECTION FINDING:

Fox Hunt:
- Locate transmitter by signal strength
- Directional antenna (Yagi, tape measure)
- Attenuator (reduce strong signals)
- Triangulation from multiple positions

Counter-DF:
- Low power (harder to detect at distance)
- Short transmissions
- Terrain masking
- Frequency agility

4. COMPUTER SECURITY
====================

OPERATING SYSTEMS:

Secure OS:
- Tails: Amnesic, Tor-based, live USB
- Qubes: Compartmentalized VMs
- Whonix: Tor isolation
- Linux: More secure than Windows (generally)

Full Disk Encryption:
- LUKS (Linux)
- BitLocker (Windows)
- FileVault (Mac)
- VeraCrypt (cross-platform, open source)

Plausible Deniability:
- Hidden volumes (VeraCrypt)
- Two passwords: Real data vs decoy data
- Can't prove hidden volume exists

PASSWORD SECURITY:

Strong Passwords:
- 16+ characters
- Mix: Upper, lower, numbers, symbols
- Unique per account
- Password manager (KeePassXC, Bitwarden)

Passphrase Method:
- "correct horse battery staple"
- 4-6 random words = strong + memorable
- Dice method (Diceware)

2FA (Two-Factor Authentication):
- TOTP apps (Aegis, Authy)
- NOT SMS (SIM swapping)
- Hardware keys (YubiKey, Titan)

SECURE COMMUNICATIONS:

Encrypted Messaging:
- Signal: End-to-end, open source, best
- Threema: Swiss, paid, no phone number
- Briar: P2P, works without internet
- Session: Onion-routed, anonymous

Email:
- PGP/GPG encryption
- ProtonMail (end-to-end)
- Tutanota
- Self-hosted (advanced)

VPN:
- Hides IP from websites
- Doesn't hide from ISP that you're using VPN
- No logs policy critical
- Mullvad, IVPN, ProtonVPN
- NOT free VPNs (sell data)

Tor:
- Onion routing (3 hops)
- Anonymous browsing
- Access .onion sites
- Slow but very private
- Use Tor Browser (pre-configured)

METADATA:

What is it:
- Data about data
- Who, when, where (not what)
- Email: Sender, recipient, time, subject
- Photo: GPS, camera model, timestamp

Removal:
- ExifTool (photos)
- mat2 (documents)
- Metadata Cleaner (GUI)

Prevention:
- Don't create it (screenshot vs photo)
- Disposable cameras
- No GPS on photos

5. NETWORK HACKING
====================

⚠️ ILLEGAL WITHOUT AUTHORIZATION
⚠️ Your network or authorized pentest ONLY

RECONNAISSANCE:

Nmap (Port Scanning):
```bash
# Basic scan
nmap 192.168.1.0/24

# Service detection
nmap -sV 192.168.1.1

# OS detection
nmap -O 192.168.1.1

# All TCP ports
nmap -p- 192.168.1.1

# Stealth scan
nmap -sS 192.168.1.1
```

Wireshark (Packet Capture):
- Captures all network traffic
- Filter by protocol, IP, port
- Unencrypted data visible
- HTTPS encrypted (can't read content)

MITM (Man-in-the-Middle):

ARP Spoofing:
- Tool: arpspoof, ettercap
- Intercept traffic between router and target
- Can modify packets in transit
- Defeated by static ARP entries

Evil Twin (WiFi):
- Fake access point with same SSID
- Capture credentials when users connect
- Tool: airbase-ng, hostapd
- Defeated by WPA2-Enterprise

WIRELESS ATTACKS:

WEP Cracking (Obsolete):
- Capture IV packets (aircrack-ng)
- 5-10 minutes to crack
- WEP should never be used

WPA2 PSK Cracking:
- Capture 4-way handshake
- Offline dictionary/brute force attack
- hashcat, aircrack-ng
- Strong password = infeasible

Tools:
```bash
# Monitor mode
airmon-ng start wlan0

# Capture handshake
airodump-ng -c 6 --bssid XX:XX:XX:XX:XX:XX -w capture wlan0mon

# Deauth clients (force reconnect)
aireplay-ng -0 10 -a XX:XX:XX:XX:XX:XX wlan0mon

# Crack handshake
aircrack-ng -w wordlist.txt capture.cap
```

WPS Attack:
- WPS PIN brute force (Reaver)
- Some routers vulnerable
- Takes 4-10 hours
- Defeated by disabling WPS

EVIL MAID ATTACK:

Physical Access:
- Boot from USB (live OS)
- Mount encrypted drive
- Install keylogger/backdoor
- User enters password → captured

Defenses:
- BIOS password
- Disable USB boot
- Tamper-evident seals
- Full disk encryption with TPM

PASSWORD CRACKING:

Tools:
- John the Ripper
- Hashcat (GPU-accelerated)
- Hydra (online brute force)

Methods:
- Dictionary attack (wordlist)
- Rule-based (l33t speak mutations)
- Brute force (all combinations)
- Rainbow tables (pre-computed hashes)

Hash Types:
- MD5: Broken, fast to crack
- SHA1: Broken, fast to crack
- SHA256: Secure but no salt
- bcrypt: Designed to be slow (good)
- scrypt: Memory-hard (better)
- Argon2: Current best practice

EXPLOITATION:

Metasploit Framework:
- Exploit database
- Payload generation
- Post-exploitation
- Requires known vulnerability

Web Vulnerabilities:
- SQL Injection: ' OR '1'='1
- XSS (Cross-Site Scripting)
- CSRF (Cross-Site Request Forgery)
- Directory traversal: ../../etc/passwd
- File upload (reverse shell)

Social Engineering:
- Phishing emails
- Pretexting (fake identity)
- Baiting (infected USB)
- Tailgating (physical)
- Most effective attack vector

6. PHYSICAL SECURITY BYPASS
====================

RFID CLONING:

HID Prox Cards (125 kHz):
- Proxmark3 (reader/cloner)
- Read card, copy to blank
- Very easy (no encryption)
- T5577 blank cards

MIFARE Classic (13.56 MHz):
- Proxmark3 or Flipper Zero
- Nested attack or hardnested
- Some keys default (manufacturer)
- Crack in minutes

MIFARE DESFire/Plus:
- Encrypted, very difficult
- Requires knowing keys
- Not easily cloneable

Badge Readers:

Wiegand Protocol:
- Simple parallel data
- Red + green + ground wires
- Data visible in clear
- Easy to tap (ESPKey, Tastic RFID Thief)

BADGE RELAY ATTACK:

Setup:
- Reader at door
- Transmitter near victim's badge
- Relay signal = door opens
- Distance can be meters

Defense:
- Metal wallet (Faraday cage)
- RFID blocking sleeves
- Active cards (button press required)

MAGSTRIPE CLONING:

Readers:
- MSR605, MSR206
- Read magnetic stripe
- Write to blank cards

Credit Cards:
- Track 1, 2, 3 data
- CVV on stripe (not same as CVV2 on card)
- Can clone to blank
- ⚠️ Fraud laws VERY serious

Hotel Keys:
- Usually RFID now
- Old magstripe easy to clone

ACCESS CONTROL:

Default Credentials:
- Many systems ship with defaults
- admin/admin, admin/password, root/root
- Check manufacturer documentation
- Shodan.io (search exposed systems)

Backdoors:
- Master codes programmed
- "Service" codes
- Engineer menus (special key combo)

Tailgating:
- Follow authorized person
- Social engineering ("hands full")
- Wearing uniform/badge (fake or real)
- Confidence is key

7. SURVEILLANCE & COUNTER-SURVEILLANCE
====================

SURVEILLANCE DEVICES:

GPS Trackers:
- Magnetic mount (vehicle)
- Battery: Days to months
- Real-time or logging
- Detection: Physical search, RF detector

Audio Bugs:
- Wired (parasitic power from phone line)
- Wireless (RF transmission)
- GSM (SIM card, cell network)
- Digital recorders (no transmission)

Video:
- Pinhole cameras (very small)
- WiFi cameras
- Dummy cameras (deterrent)

DETECTION:

RF Detector:
- Finds wireless transmissions
- Sweep room while transmitter active
- Phone call = activate audio bug
- Cheap detectors: $50-200
- Pro: $500-5000

Non-Linear Junction Detector (NLJD):
- Finds electronics (on or off)
- Detects semiconductor junctions
- Very expensive ($10k+)
- Used by professionals

Physical Search:
- Systematic room search
- Check outlets, fixtures, decorations
- Thermal imaging (cameras generate heat)
- Disassemble suspicious items

COUNTER-SURVEILLANCE:

White Noise:
- Sound masking
- Prevents audio surveillance
- Ultrasonic jammer (microphones)

Faraday Cage:
- Blocks all RF in/out
- DIY: Metal screen room
- Phone Faraday bag (blocks GPS, cell)

TEMPEST:
- Electromagnetic emanations
- CRT monitors emit signal (can be reconstructed)
- Air gaps can leak data
- Shielding required (expensive)

CAMERA EVASION:

IR LEDs:
- Infrared array on hat
- Blinds cameras (some)
- Visible at night
- Not effective on all cameras

Facial Recognition Defeat:
- Asymmetric makeup (CV Dazzle)
- IR reflective glasses
- Face masks (now common)
- Tilted head, hat
- Not 100% effective

Gait Analysis:
- Walking pattern recognition
- Harder to defeat (unconscious)
- Limp, different shoes, pebble in shoe
- Emerging technology

8. IMPROVISED ELECTRONICS
====================

BATTERY ALTERNATIVES:

Lemon Battery:
- Zinc + Copper electrodes
- Lemon/potato/salt water electrolyte
- ~0.9V per cell
- Series for higher voltage
- Low current (milliamps)

Earth Battery:
- Copper + zinc/aluminum rods in ground
- Moist soil electrolyte
- 0.5-1V per cell
- Can power small devices
- Semi-permanent installation

CRYSTAL RADIO (No power needed):

Components:
- Coil (inductor): Wire wrapped around tube
- Variable capacitor (tuner)
- Diode (germanium best, 1N34A)
- Antenna (long wire)
- Ground (metal rod in earth)
- High-impedance headphones

How it works:
- Antenna captures AM radio waves
- LC circuit resonates at frequency (tuning)
- Diode rectifies (demodulates)
- Headphones convert to sound
- Powered entirely by radio wave energy

SPARK GAP TRANSMITTER (Historical):

⚠️ ILLEGAL - Creates massive interference

Components:
- High voltage source (ignition coil)
- Spark gap (two wires/balls close)
- Antenna
- Telegraph key

Operation:
- Key closes circuit
- Spark jumps gap
- Radiates RF broadly
- Very crude, wideband interference
- Used before vacuum tubes (1900s-1920s)

JOULE THIEF (Voltage Booster):

Circuit:
- Single transistor (2N3904)
- Toroid inductor (ferrite bead + wire)
- Resistor (1kΩ)
- LED + dead battery (0.5V+)

Function:
- Boosts voltage from dead battery
- Can light LED from "dead" AA cell
- Uses magnetic energy storage
- Very simple, useful circuit

EMP PROTECTION:

Faraday Cage:
- Conductive enclosure (no gaps)
- Galvanized trash can with lid
- Aluminum foil (multiple layers)
- Copper mesh
- Ground not necessary (debated)

What to protect:
- Radios, computers, electronics
- Power supplies
- Solar charge controllers
- Spare devices

Effectiveness:
- Commercial EMP = very strong
- Faraday cage helps but not 100%
- Multiple layers better
- Nested cages (cage within cage)

9. SIGNAL INTELLIGENCE (SIGINT)
====================

TRAFFIC ANALYSIS:

Pattern of Life:
- Radio usage times
- Frequency patterns
- Transmission lengths
- Network mapping
- No decryption needed

Direction Finding:
- Multiple receivers (3+)
- Triangulate transmitter location
- Time Difference of Arrival (TDOA)
- Frequency Difference of Arrival (FDOA)

INTERCEPT:

HF Intercept:
- Skywave propagation
- Can intercept 1000+ km away
- Morse code easier than voice
- USB/LSB mode for SSB

VHF/UHF Intercept:
- Line of sight
- Repeaters extend range
- Trunked radio (complex)
- Digital modes need decoders

Satellite:
- Geostationary (fixed position)
- LEO (Low Earth Orbit, moves)
- Satellite phone intercept possible
- Iridium, Inmarsat
- Expensive equipment required

DECRYPTION:

Weak Encryption:
- Commercial voice scramblers (breakable)
- Analog inversion (trivial)
- DES (broken)
- Export-grade crypto (weakened)

Strong Encryption:
- AES-256 (unbreakable currently)
- Modern algorithms (properly implemented)
- Side-channel attacks possible
- Implementation flaws exploitable

Cryptanalysis:
- Known plaintext attack
- Chosen plaintext attack
- Frequency analysis (classical ciphers)
- Requires expertise + time

10. ELECTRONIC WARFARE
====================

⚠️ ILLEGAL IN MOST COUNTRIES
⚠️ INFORMATIONAL PURPOSES ONLY

JAMMING:

Noise Jamming:
- Transmit random noise on frequency
- Raises noise floor
- Prevents communication
- Simple but effective
- Continuous or pulsed

Spot Jamming:
- Target specific frequency
- High power on narrow band
- Very effective
- Easy to detect

Barrage Jamming:
- Wide frequency range
- Lower power density
- Harder to avoid
- Requires more power

GPS Jamming:
- GPS L1 frequency: 1575.42 MHz
- Very weak signal (-130 dBm)
- Easy to jam
- Illegal ($100k+ fine USA)

Detection:
- Spectrum analyzer
- Directional antenna
- Direction finding
- Locate jammer

Counter-Jamming:
- Frequency hopping
- Spread spectrum
- Directional antennas
- Higher power
- Different frequency band

DENIAL OF SERVICE:

WiFi Deauth:
- 802.11 management frames
- Kick users off network
- aireplay-ng, mdk3
- Does NOT require password
- Trivial to execute

Bluetooth Jamming:
- 2.4 GHz band
- Interferes with WiFi too
- Ubertooth One (tool)

Cell Jamming:
- VERY illegal
- Multiple bands (700-2100 MHz)
- FCC extremely serious enforcement
- Prison time

IMSI CATCHER (Stingray):

Function:
- Fake cell tower
- Phones connect (strongest signal)
- Intercept calls, SMS, data
- Track location

Legality:
- Law enforcement use (warrant required)
- Civilian possession illegal (USA)
- Available commercially (other countries)

Detection:
- CatcherCatcher app (Android)
- Monitor cell tower changes
- Sudden signal strength changes
- Downgrade to 2G (suspicious)
