WATER PURIFICATION & SOURCING GUIDE
Critical Survival Skill

====================
WATER PRIORITY
====================

Rule of 3s:
- 3 minutes without air = death
- 3 hours without shelter (extreme weather) = death
- 3 DAYS without water = death
- 3 weeks without food = death

Human needs: 2-4 liters per day (more if hot/active)

====================
WATER SOURCES
====================

SAFE (Usually):
✓ Springs
✓ Deep wells
✓ Rainwater (collected cleanly)
✓ Snow/ice (melted)

TREAT BEFORE USE:
⚠️ Rivers, streams
⚠️ Lakes, ponds
⚠️ Shallow wells
⚠️ Rainwater (roof collection)

AVOID:
❌ Stagnant water
❌ Water with dead animals
❌ Algae-covered water
❌ Chemical smell/taste
❌ Oil sheen (gasoline, etc.)
❌ Foam (detergents)
❌ Agricultural runoff
❌ Industrial areas
❌ Downstream of cities

====================
PURIFICATION METHODS
====================

1. BOILING (Most Reliable)

Kills: ALL pathogens (bacteria, viruses, parasites)
Doesn't remove: Chemicals, heavy metals, sediment

Procedure:
1. Filter sediment first (cloth)
2. Rolling boil:
   - Sea level: 1 minute
   - Above 2000m: 3 minutes
   - Above 3000m: 5 minutes
3. Cool before drinking
4. Aerate if flat taste (pour between containers)

Fuel conservation:
- Heat rocks in fire
- Drop hot rocks in water (boils without continuous fire)
- SODIS method first (see below)

2. CHEMICAL TREATMENT

BLEACH (Sodium Hypochlorite):

Unscented bleach only (5.25% NaClO)
- Clear water: 8 drops per gallon (2 drops per liter)
- Cloudy water: 16 drops per gallon (4 drops per liter)
- Wait 30 minutes minimum
- Should smell slightly of chlorine
- If no smell: Add same amount again, wait 15 more minutes

Pool Shock (Calcium Hypochlorite):
- More concentrated
- Longer shelf life (years vs months)
- Mix: 1 teaspoon per 2 gallons water = bleach solution
- Then use bleach solution as above
- Store powder dry, cool

IODINE:

Tincture (2%):
- 5 drops per liter
- Wait 30 minutes
- 1 hour if water very cold/cloudy

Tablets (Potable Aqua, etc.):
- Follow package directions
- Usually 1-2 tablets per liter
- 30 minute wait

Limitations:
- Doesn't kill Cryptosporidium well
- Not for pregnant women (thyroid)
- Tastes bad (Vitamin C after to improve)

CHLORINE DIOXIDE (Aquamira, etc.):

- Two-part system (mix before use)
- Kills everything including Crypto
- 30 minute wait (4 hours for Crypto)
- No bad taste
- More expensive

3. FILTRATION

COMMERCIAL FILTERS:

Gravity Filters (Berkey, etc.):
- Pour water in top
- Gravity pulls through filter
- No pumping needed
- High capacity
- Removes bacteria, parasites, sediment
- Some remove viruses (smaller pores)

Pump Filters (Katadyn, MSR, etc.):
- Manual pumping
- Fast
- Good for groups
- Field maintainable

Straw Filters (LifeStraw, Sawyer Mini):
- Personal use
- Drink directly from source
- No chemicals, no pumping
- Limited capacity
- Can't store filtered water

Filter Ratings:
- 0.1 micron: Bacteria, parasites
- 0.02 micron: Viruses
- Activated carbon: Chemicals, taste

⚠️ Filters don't remove viruses unless specifically rated
⚠️ Filters can clog (turbid water)
⚠️ Freezing can crack filter (ruin)

DIY FILTER:

Slow Sand Filter:
1. Container with drainage (bucket with holes)
2. Layers (bottom to top):
   - Gravel (2-4 inches)
   - Coarse sand (6 inches)
   - Fine sand (12 inches)
   - Cloth on top (prevent disturbance)
3. Pour water slowly on top
4. Collect from bottom
5. First few liters = discard (flush system)
6. Flow should be SLOW (hours for gallon)

Biosand Filter:
- Same as above but:
- Biological layer develops on top sand
- Microorganisms eat pathogens
- Takes 1-2 weeks to establish
- Very effective when mature

Charcoal Filter:
1. Crush charcoal finely
2. Layer in container:
   - Cloth
   - Sand (2")
   - Charcoal (4-6")
   - Sand (2")
   - Gravel (2")
   - Cloth
3. Slow drip through
4. Removes chemicals, improves taste
5. NOT sufficient alone for pathogens

⚠️ DIY filters improve water but DON'T guarantee safety
⚠️ Boil or chemically treat filtered water if possible

4. UV TREATMENT

SteriPEN / UV Wands:
- Ultraviolet light damages DNA
- 30-90 seconds per liter
- Requires batteries
- Clear water only (turbidity blocks UV)
- Effective when working correctly

SODIS (Solar Disinfection):
1. Clear plastic bottle (PET)
2. Fill with water (pre-filter if cloudy)
3. Place in direct sunlight
4. 6 hours (sunny day) or 2 days (cloudy)
5. UV + heat kills pathogens
6. FREE method

Requirements:
- Bottle must be clear (no label)
- PET plastic (check recycling symbol)
- Direct sun (not through window)
- Water not too turbid

5. DISTILLATION

Process:
- Boil water → Collect steam → Condense to liquid
- Result: Pure H2O (no pathogens, chemicals, salts)
- Only method for saltwater/heavily contaminated

Solar Still:
1. Dig hole (3 ft wide, 2 ft deep)
2. Place container in center
3. Place vegetation around container (moisture source)
4. Cover hole with plastic sheet
5. Place rock in center (above container)
6. Seal edges with dirt/sand
7. Sun heats → water evaporates → condenses on plastic → drips to container

Yield: 1-2 liters per day (if vegetation available)

Distillation Still (Fire-based):
1. Pot with water (contaminated)
2. Lid inverted (condenser)
3. Heat pot
4. Steam condenses on lid
5. Drips to collection (tube or tip of lid)

⚠️ Fuel-intensive
⚠️ Slow
⚠️ But produces absolutely pure water

====================
SEDIMENT REMOVAL
====================

Pre-filtering extends life of chemical/filters:

Cloth Filter:
- Bandana, t-shirt, coffee filter
- Multiple layers
- Pour through slowly
- Removes visible particles only

Settling:
1. Fill container with turbid water
2. Add alum (aluminum sulfate) - optional
3. Let sit 12-24 hours
4. Particles settle to bottom
5. Carefully pour/siphon clear water from top

Alum flocculation:
- 1/4 teaspoon per gallon
- Stir vigorously
- Particles clump (flocculate)
- Settle faster

====================
WATER INDICATORS
====================

FINDING WATER:

Animal tracks:
- Converging trails → water source
- Fresh tracks (recent rain)
- Birds fly toward water (morning/evening)

Vegetation:
- Green vegetation in dry area
- Willows, cottonwoods (need water)
- Cattails, reeds (standing water)

Terrain:
- Valley bottoms
- Base of cliffs (springs)
- Dry stream beds (dig in lowest spot)

Digging for water:
1. Lowest point of dry stream
2. Dig 1-2 feet
3. If sand damp, keep digging
4. Water will seep in (muddy at first)
5. Let settle, then purify

====================
RAINWATER COLLECTION
====================

Direct Collection:
- Tarps, ponchos (funnel to container)
- Containers placed in open
- Roof gutters (if building available)

First Flush:
- First rain washes roof/air pollution
- Discard first few minutes of rain
- Then collect

Storage:
- Covered containers (prevent mosquitoes)
- Dark storage (prevent algae)
- Use within days (or purify)

====================
EMERGENCY SOURCES
====================

Dew Collection:
- Tie cloth around ankles
- Walk through grass at dawn
- Wring out cloth into container
- Laborious but works

Transpiration Bag:
1. Clear plastic bag
2. Tie around living green branch
3. Sun causes leaves to transpire
4. Water collects in bag
5. Collect after 4-6 hours
6. 1-2 cups possible

Vegetation Water:
- Vines (cut, water drips out)
- Bamboo (shake, water in nodes)
- Barrel cactus (emergency only, hard to process)

⚠️ These methods VERY inefficient
⚠️ Calories burned > water gained often
⚠️ Absolute last resort

====================
TESTING WATER QUALITY
====================

Visual:
- Clear > cloudy
- No dead animals visible
- No algae
- No oil/chemical sheen

Smell:
- Odorless = better
- Chlorine smell after treatment = good
- Chemical/petroleum smell = BAD
- Sulfur smell = bacteria (not necessarily unsafe but treat)

Taste (after purification):
- Clean taste = likely good
- Metallic = minerals (usually OK)
- Chemical = contamination
- If bad taste after purification = find different source

====================
WATER STORAGE
====================

Containers:
- Food-grade plastic (HDPE #2, PETE #1)
- Glass (heavy but inert)
- Stainless steel (not aluminum)

Sanitizing containers:
1. Wash with soap
2. Rinse
3. Sanitize with bleach solution (1 tsp/quart)
4. Let sit 2 minutes
5. Rinse thoroughly

Long-term storage:
- Keep cool, dark
- Rotate every 6 months
- Add 8 drops bleach per gallon (optional)
- Sealed containers prevent recontamination

Emergency storage:
- Fill bathtub (at collapse warning)
- Water heater (40-80 gallons)
- Toilet tank (NOT bowl)

====================
CHEMICAL CONTAMINATION
====================

Common contaminants:
- Gasoline/petroleum
- Pesticides/herbicides
- Heavy metals (lead, mercury, arsenic)
- Industrial chemicals

Removal methods:
- Activated carbon (charcoal)
- Distillation (best)
- Reverse osmosis (requires equipment)

⚠️ Boiling/bleach DON'T remove chemicals
⚠️ If source chemically contaminated, find different source

====================
PATHOGENS IN WATER
====================

BACTERIA (0.5-3 microns):
- E. coli
- Salmonella
- Cholera
- Shigella

Effects: Diarrhea, vomiting, death (if severe)
Killed by: Boiling, bleach, iodine, 0.1μ filters

VIRUSES (0.02-0.3 microns):
- Hepatitis A
- Norovirus
- Rotavirus
- Polio

Effects: Varies (liver damage, diarrhea, etc.)
Killed by: Boiling, bleach (if enough), iodine, distillation
NOT removed by most filters (too small)

PARASITES (5-50 microns):
- Giardia (cysts)
- Cryptosporidium (oocysts)
- Amoebas

Effects: Severe diarrhea, malnutrition
Killed by: Boiling, distillation, filtration
RESISTANT to bleach/iodine (Crypto especially)

⚠️ Diarrhea from bad water → dehydration → death
⚠️ Treat ALL suspect water

====================
QUANTITIES NEEDED
====================

Per person per day:
- Drinking: 2 liters minimum
- Cooking: 2-4 liters
- Hygiene: 5+ liters
- Total: 10-15 liters ideal

Survival minimum: 2 liters/day
Rationing: 500ml every 8 hours (bare minimum)

⚠️ Dehydration symptoms:
- Dark urine
- Headache
- Dizziness
- Dry mouth
- Weakness
